The Historical Visual Archive of the Kohli Samaj serves as a curated photographic, architectural, and documentary catalog preserving tangible records of the community across eastern Vidarbha. Organized systematically across thematic domains—ranging from centuries-old Malguzari reservoir embankments to pioneering twentieth-century community conventions—the archive links visual material directly to primary colonial gazetteers and verified community records.

Consistent with scientific archival methodologies, this repository maintains an explicit provenance policy: photographs and site records are accompanied by administrative citations, geographic locations, and source annotations. Where archival photographic plates are pending official digitisation, the repository utilizes descriptive catalog cards rather than speculative or unverified imagery.

Village Architecture & Rural Settlements

Historic Kohli settlements were characterized by compact spatial organization situated adjacent to irrigation tanks, featuring prominent Malguzari residences (Wadas or Gadhis), wide threshing floors, and mud-and-timber residences built with indigenous thermal insulation.

Water Heritage & Historic Reservoirs

The hydraulic monuments constructed by Kohli Malguzars constitute one of central India's most extraordinary irrigation legacies, encompassing thousands of interconnected water bodies such as Navegaon Bandh and Bodalkasa.

Image Verification & Archival Submission Policy

Strict Provenance Standards:

Historical photographs and documents featured on this portal are published exclusively with authenticated source provenance, date, and geographic context. The repository strictly excludes unverified internet screenshots, speculative digital mockups, and AI-generated portraits to protect the scientific integrity of community records.
