Image Verification & Provenance Policy

Historical photographs are published with available source information. Where the date, location or identity cannot yet be established, the gallery clearly marks the information as undocumented rather than presenting an assumption as fact.

The gallery only accepts authenticated community photographs, supplied archival materials, or public-domain historical records. Unverified AI-generated historical portraits and unofficial internet screenshots are strictly excluded.
