Chanda Settlement Report — Historical Reference to the Kohli Community

A historical settlement report by A. B. Napier, Settlement Officer, provides valuable observations about the Kohli community's agricultural practices, sugarcane cultivation, traditional water management and community organization.

Kohli Agriculture and Sugarcane Cultivation

According to the settlement report, the Kohli community was strongly associated with agriculture, with particular emphasis on sugarcane cultivation. The report notes that sugarcane was especially cultivated in areas where the terrain supported the construction of large water tanks.

Traditional Water Management

One of the notable observations in the report concerns the construction of large reservoirs to retain water for irrigation during the long hot months. This historical account provides an important connection between Kohli agricultural life and traditional water-management practices.

Historical Traditions About Community Origin

The report states that the origin of the Kohli community was uncertain. It records differing traditions concerning their ancestry. One tradition mentioned a connection with Bengal, while Colonel Lucie Smith, Settlement Officer of Chanda, recorded a different view suggesting a southern origin.

Historical note: These are competing traditions recorded in the historical source. They should not be presented as a conclusively established modern origin story.
