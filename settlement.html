Historical Settlement and Land Administration Records

Historical Administrative Source

Settlement Reports were prepared to document land, revenue, villages, agricultural practices and local administrative arrangements. They provide valuable historical context for understanding rural society and land administration in the Central Provinces and adjoining regions.

How to read Settlement Reports

Settlement Reports were administrative documents created for the land and revenue systems of their time. Their terminology and classifications reflect historical administrative practices. They are valuable historical sources but should not automatically be interpreted as modern legal or social classifications.

Chanda District Settlement Report

19th Century Chanda (Chandrapur) Settlement Report

The Chanda Settlement Report is an important historical administrative source for understanding land settlement, agriculture, village administration and rural society in the Chanda region.

Kohli References in the Chanda Settlement Material

Supplied historical material contains references to the Kohli/Kohri community in the Chanda region. The material describes their association with agriculture, sugarcane cultivation and village-level social organization.

Historical clarification: Some historical records describe particular families or localities with specific administrative involvement. These records reflect historical administrative roles in specific areas rather than universal status for all community members.

Bhandara District Settlement Records

Bhandara Region Historical Administrative Record

Historical settlement records relating to Bhandara provide information about land, villages, agriculture, revenue arrangements and local administration.

Malguzari and Village Administration

Historical material from the region refers to Malguzari arrangements and village-level revenue administration. In some areas, influential landholding families and village officials played important roles in administration and revenue collection.

Administrative Context: Some Kohli families are described in supplied historical/community material as Patils or Malguzars in particular localities.

Patil and Village-Level Administration

Patil was a village-level administrative role in historical rural systems. Community accounts describe Kohli Patil families in several villages as socially influential and involved in village affairs.

Local administrative arrangements varied from village to village and across historical periods.

Land, Agriculture and Revenue

Settlement records are particularly useful for understanding how agricultural land was organized, assessed and administered.

Agricultural Land

Historical classification, surveys and records of arable lands under local cultivation.

Land Revenue

Administrative assessment structures and settlement terms established by revenue authorities.

Village Records

Village-level mapping, boundary demarcations, and landholder registries.

Agricultural Production

Crops, sugarcane farming traditions, and local cultivation arrangements.

Water Resources and Irrigation

Historical settlement material also provides context for understanding tanks, irrigation and agricultural water resources that supported rural settlements.
