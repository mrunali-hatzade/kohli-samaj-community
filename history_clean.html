Kohli Samaj — Historical Background

Historical Record

The Kohli community has a long-standing presence in the Vidarbha region and adjoining areas. Historical and community sources refer to Kohli settlements across Bhandara, Gondia, Gadchiroli, Chandrapur and Balaghat. The community is also present in urban centres such as Nagpur, Pune and Mumbai, particularly due to education, employment and business.

Historical Overview

Centuries of agricultural stewardship, community governance, and water conservation practices documented across Central Provinces administration records.

Geographic Presence

5 key districts (Bhandara, Gondia, Gadchiroli, Chandrapur, Balaghat) with 518 villages (Community-reported figure) alongside urban hubs like Nagpur, Pune, and Mumbai.

Traditional Livelihood

Historically, agriculture formed an important part of the community's economic life, associated with rice cultivation, sugarcane, and village water infrastructure.

Village Institutions

Historical accounts describe the Kohli Patil as an important figure in village affairs, involving local administration, agricultural matters, decision-making and social coordination.

Four Pillars of Kohli Heritage

Agriculture

Traditional agricultural livelihood and rural life.

Water Conservation

Tanks, reservoirs, irrigation and traditional water management.

Village Institutions

Historical village administration and leadership.

Culture & Community

Family traditions, customs, festivals, and social unity.
