Origin & Historical Journey

The Kohli community has a documented history extending over several centuries in Vidarbha, particularly in Bhandara, Chanda (Chandrapur), Gondia, and Balaghat. Historical gazetteers from the British colonial era acknowledge the Kohlis as master water engineers and hereditary Malguzars.

Traditional Agricultural Heritage (Central Provinces)

Historical records from the Central Provinces associate the Kohli community with cultivation and agriculture. Agriculture formed an important part of the community's traditional livelihood. Later historical references also describe the community's association with crops such as sugarcane and rice and with traditional water-management systems used to support agriculture.

Historical reference: Hindoo Castes in Central Provinces, p. 323.

Traditional Agricultural Heritage (Chanda Settlement)

Historical settlement records associate the Kohli community with agriculture, sugarcane cultivation and traditional water-management practices.

Kohli Agricultural Heritage

Historical Marathi references describe the Kohli community of eastern Vidarbha in connection with agriculture, rice and sugarcane cultivation, and traditional water-management practices. These accounts also describe practical knowledge used in constructing and maintaining tanks and irrigation structures.
