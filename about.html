About Kohli Samaj

Kohli Samaj is a distinguished, hardworking agricultural and water-engineering community primarily settled across Vidarbha region of Maharashtra and parts of Madhya Pradesh. Renowned historically as the builders of the famous Malguzari tanks (bhandaras), the community has contributed immensely to rural prosperity and irrigation engineering.

Core Pillars & Objectives

  • Preserving indigenous water management and tank building heritage.
  • Fostering youth education, competitive exam counseling, and financial aid.
  • Promoting community harmony through annual Vadhu-Var Parichay Melavas.
  • Unifying rural and urban community forums.
