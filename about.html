Kohli Community (कोहळी समाज)

From Wikipedia and community historical archives, the free encyclopedic record
Kohli Samaj
Vidarbha Community Overview
Official Emblem of the Kohli Community
Classification Agrarian, Tank-Architectural & Malguzari Community
Geographic Sphere Eastern Vidarbha & Southern Madhya Pradesh (518 Villages across 5 Districts)
Core Districts Bhandara, Gondia, Chandrapur, Gadchiroli, Balaghat
Urban Centers Nagpur, Pune, Mumbai
Traditional Occupations Paddy Farming, Sugarcane & Jaggery, Tank Construction, Agrarian Stewardship
Social Order Role Patrons (*poshinde*) of the Bara Balutedar system; Malguzari landholders
Traditional Deities Kolasur, Tadoba, Rama, Shiva, Bhairava, Maruti, Kholyakholba, Kali Devi, Sat Devi
Key Engineering Works Navegaon Bandh, Ambazari Tank (Nagpur), thousands of Malguzari bodis
Languages Marathi, Regional Kohali dialect
