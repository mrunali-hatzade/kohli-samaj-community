Historical Census Records

Demographic Archive

Historical census publications provide demographic information from different periods. Administrative boundaries and classification systems changed over time, so historical figures must always be read together with their year, district and source.

1911 Bhandara District Historical Population Record

1911 Census — Bhandara District

Source: Bhandara District / Central Provinces District Gazetteer statistical material
11,399
Kohli Population

According to the supplied historical statistical material, the Kohli population recorded in Bhandara District in the 1911 census was 11,399.

Important Note: This is a historical figure based on the administrative boundaries of Bhandara District at that time. It should not be directly compared with the present-day Bhandara district.

Historical Administrative Boundaries

Historical districts and tehsils did not always have the same boundaries as today's administrative units. Changes in district formation, tehsil boundaries and administrative arrangements can therefore produce different population figures in different historical records.
