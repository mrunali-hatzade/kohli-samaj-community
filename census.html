Historical Population & Origin Records

Kohli Community in Historical Records

R. V. Russell's historical account records the Kohlis or Kohris in the districts of Bhandara and Chanda and provides population figures for the period covered by the work. The account also describes the community's association with agricultural regions where tanks supported the cultivation of crops such as sugarcane and rice.

Historical Traditions About Origin

Historical sources record differing traditions about the origins of the Kohli community. R. V. Russell records one theory connecting the community's movement to Chanda with the Benares region and the Bhonsla period, while also noting that the Chanda Kohlis themselves did not retain a tradition linking their ancestry to Upper India. These accounts should therefore be understood as historical traditions rather than a single conclusively established origin.

Historical note: These are competing traditions recorded in the historical source. They should not be presented as a conclusively established modern origin story.
