Kohli Samaj — Water Conservation Heritage

Historical Heritage

Traditional Kohli communities were historically associated with agriculture, irrigation, tanks, reservoirs and water-management practices across Vidarbha. The network of Malguzari tanks and embankments transformed the region into a thriving paddy belt.

Traditional Water Management

Construction of Tanks & Reservoirs

Building earthen dams and embankments to harvest monsoon runoff for multi-season agricultural use.

Irrigation Systems & Canals

Designing gravity-fed channels to deliver water directly to sugarcane and paddy fields.

Community Participation & Maintenance

Annual desilting, repair of bunds, and equitable water distribution managed collectively by villagers.

Traditional Engineering Knowledge

"Historical accounts describe traditional water-management knowledge and engineering practices among communities associated with the region."

Without modern surveying equipment, community elders identified natural catchment contours, spillways, and soil types to construct enduring reservoirs that sustained agriculture for centuries.
