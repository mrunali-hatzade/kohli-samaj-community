Water Conservation Heritage of the Kohli Community

Traditional Water Management, Malguzari Tanks & Indigenous Irrigation Architecture
Kohli Water Architecture
Indigenous Irrigation & Embankment Engineering
Emblem honoring centuries of indigenous water harvesting and tank building
Cultural Motto 'Samaj Tithe Talav' (Where there is the community, there is a tank)
Core Principle 'Paani Adva, Paani Jirva' (Communal self-reliance without state grants)
Monumental Works Navegaon Bandh, Ambazari (Nagpur), Bodalkasa, Asolamendha, Seoni Bandh
Key Engineering Features Earthen Bunds (*Pal*), Stone Sluices (*Khind/Thana*), Waste Weirs (*Sandwa*), Contour Channels
Primary Irrigated Crops Fragrant Rice (Luchai, Chinnor, Dubraj), Sugarcane, Jaggery
Primary Geographic Basin Wainganga River Basin (Bhandara, Gondia, Chandrapur, Gadchiroli, Balaghat)
Archival Recognition Central Provinces District Gazetteers (Bhandara 1908, Chanda 1909)
