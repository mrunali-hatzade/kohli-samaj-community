Historical Names आणि संदर्भ

Historical publications may record the Kohli community under different spellings and forms, reflecting the terminology and transliteration practices of their period. References include forms such as Kohiree, Kohulee, Koholee, Kohoree, Kohri and Kohli.

Historical Spelling Source Publication Year Page / Reference
Kohiree Gazetteer of the Central Provinces 1867 Volume I, p. 235
Kohulee Census of India 1881 1881 Table III, p. 112
Koholee Census of India 1891 1891 Table VIII, p. 94
Kohoree Census of India 1901 1901 Table XIV, p. 104
Kohri R. V. Russell - The Tribes & Castes 1916 p. 176
Kohli Census of India 1911 / Chanda Settlement 1911 / 1870 p. 323 / Chanda Report
Kaulees Maharashtra State Gazetteers: Chandrapur 1909 / 1973 Revised Edition, p. 182
Note: Historical source records identify these alternate forms and spellings in relation to historical records of the community. They are reproduced here for research and context, and should not be automatically assumed to refer to the exact same modern community in every context.
