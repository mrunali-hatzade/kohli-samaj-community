Maharashtra State Gazetteers and earlier regional records provide valuable historical information about the communities, settlements, occupations and social life of the Vidarbha region. These records are an important part of understanding the historical context of the Kohli Samaj.

Historical Gazetteer Reference

Maharashtra State Gazetteers — Chandrapur District

Historical Reference

The Chandrapur District Gazetteer contains historical references to communities associated with the region. One historical excerpt refers to communities including Manas, Gowarees and 'Kaulees'. The reference is presented here as a historical record and should be understood in the context of the period in which the Gazetteer was compiled.

First Edition
1909
Revised Edition
1973
Region
Chandrapur District, Maharashtra

Historical References to Kohli / Kaulee

Historical records sometimes use different spellings or forms of community names. Such variations are important when studying older records because terminology and classification systems changed over time.

Historical Spelling
Kaulees
Source: Maharashtra State Gazetteers — Chandrapur District
Editions: 1909 / Revised 1973
Context: Historical reference to communities of the Chandrapur region.

Note: Historical spellings and terminology are reproduced for archival and research context and should not be interpreted as contemporary social classifications.

Historical Gazetteer References

Regional Gazetteers contain historical references to communities of Chandrapur and Vidarbha, including historical spellings such as 'Kaulees'. These references are preserved as part of the region's historical record.

Why These Historical Records Matter

Gazetteers provide a window into the historical geography, communities, occupations and social life of earlier periods. For the Kohli Samaj, such records help researchers and community members trace historical references, understand regional terminology and preserve knowledge for future generations.
