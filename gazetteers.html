Historical Gazetteers

British-era district gazetteers documenting Kohli Samaj as master water engineers and hereditary Malguzars of Vidarbha

What are Gazetteers?

British-era official reference books documenting geography, history, caste groups, economy, and administration of each district in Colonial India.

Why are they Important?

Gazetteers are the most authoritative historical record. Their mention of Kohli Malguzars validates the community's centuries-old role as tank-builders and landlords.

Kohli Legacy Documented

All 5 gazetteers consistently recognize the Kohli community as the primary builders and maintainers of Vidarbha's 3,000+ tank irrigation network.
