Historical Reference & Archive

Administrative Archive

Historical Gazetteers are important sources for understanding the social, agricultural, administrative and economic life of regions during earlier periods. References to Kohli/Kohri communities occur in historical publications associated with the Central Provinces and later Maharashtra regions.

This section presents these references as historical source material and does not interpret historical terminology as a modern legal or constitutional classification.

How to read historical Gazetteer references

Gazetteers were written for the administrative and scholarly purposes of their time. Their terminology, classifications and descriptions reflect the period in which they were produced. Historical references on this website are presented for historical and educational purposes and should not automatically be interpreted as present-day legal, constitutional or social classifications.

Historical Government References

Kohli/Kohri references can be found within historical government and administrative publications relating to the regions of Vidarbha and adjoining Central Provinces.

Administration
