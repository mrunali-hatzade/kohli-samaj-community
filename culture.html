Culture, Traditions & Festivals

Pola, Tansen, traditional agrarian festivals, kuldaivats, and vibrant social customs

Vibrant Traditions & Community Values

The Kohli Samaj celebrates rich cultural festivals reflecting deep agricultural roots and respect for nature. From Pola (worship of farm cattle) to Diwali and traditional folk music dances, our traditions foster strong unity.

Key Traditions:
  • Bail Pola Festival & Agricultural Gratitude
  • Vadhu-Var Parichay Melava (Matrimonial Conventions)
  • Folk Songs & Village Cultural Gatherings

Traditional Community Life & Agriculture

Traditional community life has always been centered around agriculture and cultivation. As documented in early regional records, the community’s heritage is rooted in cultivating crops like sugarcane and rice, supported by their indigenous water-management networks. This agricultural legacy shapes the community's values and cultural celebrations today.

Traditional Marriage & Community Customs

Historical Cultural Practice

Historical accounts of the Kohli community describe traditional marriage ceremonies, community participation and the involvement of elders in social and family matters. These customs formed part of the community's historical cultural life and varied over time and across regions.

Note: These customs are historical references and should not be understood as mandatory rules for the modern community.
