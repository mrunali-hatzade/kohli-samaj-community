Origin & Historical Journey

The Kohali community has a documented history extending over several centuries in Vidarbha, particularly in Bhandara, Chanda (Chandrapur), Gondia, and Balaghat. Historical gazetteers from the British colonial era acknowledge the Kohalis as master water engineers and hereditary Malguzars.
